      Daily routine

        Tap any step to start from there.

        Breath

        Ready

        Farinelli breathing. Breathe in low through the nose, hold with an open throat, then hiss “sss” out evenly. Each round is one count longer. Shoulders stay still; your belly and sides move.

        0.0seconds

        One full breath, then a steady “sss” (or one note on “ah”) for as long as it stays even. Stop before you squeeze. Many teachers treat 30 seconds of steady hiss as solid support.

            Brave reps

            Full voice, with people

            You already sing well alone, so the skill is there. Getting quiet around people is self-monitoring, and it fades with exposure. Climb one rung at a time at full volume. Three reps clear a rung.
